# Weekly Async Update — Template

> **How to use:** post this format every Friday (or your team's chosen day) in the
> team channel or a docs page. Remote teams judge "communication skills" 80% through
> artifacts exactly like this. Consistency beats brilliance: same day, same format,
> every week.

---

**TL;DR (2 lines max):** <the one thing leadership must know this week>

**Shipped**

- Open P1/P2: <n>
- Cost trend: <flat / +x% / savings from ...>

---

### The 5 rules that make this format work

1. **TL;DR first** — never make a VP scroll to find the point. (BLUF: bottom line up front.)
2. **Outcomes, not activities.** "Migrated 3 environments to remote state" not "worked on Terraform".
3. **Blockers name a person and a date.** A blocker without both is a complaint, not a blocker.
4. **Handoff notes are sacred in distributed teams.** Your end of day is someone else's start.
5. **Same day, every week.** The reliability of the update is read as the reliability of the engineer.
